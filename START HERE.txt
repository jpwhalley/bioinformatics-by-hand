GMIC605 Molecular Biology Techniques
Interactive Bioinformatics Module

Keep these files together in one folder and open index.html.
No installation, login or internet connection is required.

Work through the 8 sections in order. The main route is complete on its
own; "Go deeper" panels are optional. Each section builds a lab notebook
of what you did. It is not saved automatically: download it before
closing or refreshing a page.

All individual-level reads, genotypes and family data are synthetic.
index.html has the details.
